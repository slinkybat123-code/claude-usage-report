Limit

Get Limit

What every Claude Code chat costs, your plan limits, and your other AIs, on any phone or computer. Sign in with Google, then install the small app below on the computer where you use Claude Code.

1. Sign in

Everything lives in your account, so it works from any device, even when your computer is off.

Sign in with Google

2. Install it on your computer

Windows

An installer for Windows 10 or 11. It brings everything it needs; no administrator rights are asked for.

Download for Windows

Mac

A disk image for Apple silicon and Intel Macs on macOS 12 or later.

Download for Mac
  1. Windows: run LimitSetup.exe. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway (the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate). If your browser holds the download back, choose Keep.
  2. Mac: open the disk image, drag Limit into Applications, and open it. The first time, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
  3. Your browser then asks Is this your computer? Click Yes, it's mine. That's the only step: from then on it sends by itself.

3. On your phone

Android

The Limit app: your figures, the full dashboard, plan limits, friends, and home-screen widgets that refresh by themselves. Android 8 or later.

Download for Android

iPhone, or any phone

Open this site and sign in with the same Google account. To keep it on your home screen in Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen.

Open Limit
  1. Android: open Limit.apk from your downloads. Android asks once to allow installs from your browser: allow it, then tap Install (the app isn't from the Play Store).
  2. Open Limit and tap Open the Limit site. Sign in with the same Google account and tap Yes, it's mine, then go back to the app.

Good to know

  • No Claude password or key is asked for. The app reads Claude Code's own files and sends totals, chats and limits to your account; only you can read them.
  • Friends see only what you share with them: totals and limits, never chat titles or folders. Either of you can stop at any time.
  • Other AIs (OpenAI, OpenRouter, Claude API) are added with an admin key on the All your AIs page.
  • Dollar figures are API list prices, not a bill. On a subscription you pay your plan price.
