Set it up

  1. Windows: unzip the download somewhere it can stay, like Documents, and double-click install.cmd. Mac: unzip it, move the folder to Documents, then right-click install.command and choose Open. Both need Node.js 20 or later and help you get it.
  2. The dashboard opens in your browser. Under Apps, Pair a phone shows a QR code and a pairing code.
  3. Scan the QR code with your phone camera: Android offers the app, iPhone opens the web app. Or type the code in the app. It finds your computer from then on.
  4. Friends: under Friends on the dashboard, Share yours gives you a friend code for your friends, and you can add theirs. Friends can be on any mix of Windows, Mac, Android and iPhone.

What to know

  • No Claude password or key is ever asked for. The monitor reads Claude Code's own files on your PC, and your usage stays there.
  • Your computer has to be on for the phone to update. The monitor starts by itself when you sign in.
  • On iPhone the web app shows your figures, chats and friends; Apple does not let web apps add home-screen widgets.
  • Anyone with your dashboard's address or pairing code can see your dashboard, chat titles included. Keep them to yourself.
  • Sharing with friends is off until you turn it on, shows only totals and limits, and stops whenever you choose.
  • The app is signed with a development key, so Android asks you to confirm the install.
